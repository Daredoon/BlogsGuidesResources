- Avoid using phone early in the morning from 8:00 am - 12:00 am.
- Write at least 30 minutes of anything everyday.
- Focus and concentrate 2hr to learn something new everyday.
- Research 4hr on the topic that you have choosen.
- Move around and complete 6000 to 10000 steps.
- Do anything for 2 hour of anything you enjoy.
- If you fail to focus or do any daily reasech or writing start 30 minute timer and do it. Do'nt worry about that you have to do it for 30 minutes. Cut yourself some slack.
- If you are stuck on something, do something else for 1 hour or more before continuing that.
- Read other books and article as well for overall development. Maybe improve vocabulary or learn a new language or new skill.
